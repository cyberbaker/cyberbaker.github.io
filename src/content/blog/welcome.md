Most cyber security news is written either for specialists or for headlines. This blog sits in between.

On **Everyday security** days I take a story that's in the news and explain what actually happened, why it matters to ordinary people and businesses, and what you can do about it.

On **Deep dive** days I go further into the technical side: how an attack worked, which systems are affected and what defenders should be doing. I'll still explain the jargon as I go.

Every post links to its sources, so you can check the detail for yourself.
